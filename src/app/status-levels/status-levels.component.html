Status levels

Every compatibility report rates how well an application runs under Darling. The rating is for the specific version and report it belongs to.

    @for (level of levels; track level.status) {

Good to know

  • Reports rate what was actually tested. When in doubt, the lower rating is used and the report explains why.
  • A workaround lowers the rating (to gold at best). Settings Darling expects for a whole class of apps don't: for example, 32-bit (i386) apps need vm.mmap_min_addr set to 4096 or lower (sudo sysctl vm.mmap_min_addr=4096), and reports are rated as if that were set.
  • Read the report itself for the details of what works and what doesn't.
